For buttons and short waits: the mark turns half a turn, rests, and turns again. Because the two halves mirror each other, it always lands as a Z.

Construction

64-unit grid. Both strokes are 7 units with round ends. The halves are point-symmetric around the centre, with a 4.5-unit gap on the diagonal. Keep clear space of one stroke width on every side.
